# Attention Is All You Need (arXiv:1706.03762)

- **Paper title:** Attention Is All You Need
- **arXiv ID:** 1706.03762

## Summary (from the abstract)

The paper proposes the Transformer, a new simple network architecture for sequence transduction that is based solely on attention mechanisms and dispenses with recurrence and convolutions entirely. Dominant sequence transduction models instead rely on complex recurrent or convolutional networks that include an encoder and a decoder, and the best-performing models additionally connect the encoder and decoder through an attention mechanism. On two machine translation tasks, the Transformer is superior in quality while being more parallelizable and requiring significantly less time to train. It also generalizes well to other tasks: the authors apply it successfully to English constituency parsing, with both large and limited training data.
